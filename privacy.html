Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What we collect

Nothing by default. Neon Dash stores your progress, settings, and custom levels in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

If you create an account, we store your email address and game save data (progress, coins, ghosts, editor levels) in our database provider, Supabase, to enable cloud saves and cross-device sync. If you sign in with Discord, Discord shares your basic profile information (username and avatar) with us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We don't collect payment information.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — hosts authentication and cloud save data.
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • Discord — if you play inside Discord or sign in with Discord, Discord's own privacy policy applies to their platform data.

Your controls

You can delete your account and all associated cloud save data at any time from Settings → Account in the game. You can also clear all locally stored data by clearing your browser's site data.

Children

Neon Dash is a free game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the Discord community or the repository linked on the main page.
